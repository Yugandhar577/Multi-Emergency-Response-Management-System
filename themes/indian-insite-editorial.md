# Indian Insite Editorial

The locked-in default theme currently used by the website. It is warm, editorial, and high-contrast with premium serif/sans typography.

## Color Palette

- **Paper**: `#f5efe4` - Primary page background
- **Parchment**: `#ede4d5` - Secondary surfaces
- **Ink**: `#171009` - Primary text and dark UI
- **Brass**: `#b88b3b` - Primary accent and highlight
- **Moss**: `#35544d` - Supporting accent for balance
- **Ruby**: `#8f3528` - Alert/contrast accent
- **Mist**: `#d9d0c4` - Soft neutral border/fill

## Typography

- **Headers**: Fraunces
- **Body Text**: Manrope

## Best Used For

News platforms, editorial layouts, analytical dashboards, and premium content experiences.
